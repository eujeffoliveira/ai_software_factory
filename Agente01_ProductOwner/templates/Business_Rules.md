## Policy

- Every business rule must have a traceable source: a stakeholder statement, a regulatory document, or a named policy.
- Rules without a source are not business rules — they become Open Questions (OQ-NNN).
- Business rules describe **what the system must enforce**. They do not describe **how to implement it** — that is the Architect's domain.
- Rules marked "To Confirm" must be confirmed before Gate 1 or escalated as BLOCKING OQ-NNN.

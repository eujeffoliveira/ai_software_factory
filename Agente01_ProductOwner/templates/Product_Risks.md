## Risk Classification

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Category | scope / business / user / data / regulatory / integration |
| Impact | HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW |
| Probability | HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW |
| Status | Open / Mitigated / Accepted / Closed |

**Risk Priority = Impact × Probability**
- HIGH × HIGH = Critical — must be addressed before Gate 1
- HIGH × MEDIUM or MEDIUM × HIGH = Major — mitigation plan required
- All others = Standard — monitor and accept or mitigate

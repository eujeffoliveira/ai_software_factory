# Insight Reporting Checklist

- [ ] Executive summary answers the business question.
- [ ] Every finding has an ID.
- [ ] Every finding cites evidence or metric refs.
- [ ] Every finding has a confidence label.
- [ ] Caveats are attached to findings.
- [ ] No unsupported causal language remains.
- [ ] Every recommendation has an owner.
- [ ] Every recommendation has a validation step.
- [ ] Open questions are routed.
- [ ] Risks are classified.
